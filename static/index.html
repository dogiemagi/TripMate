Frequently Asked Questions.

What exactly is TripMate AI? ↓
TripMate AI combines the rapid speed of multimodal artificial intelligence with real-world travel curation. It builds custom, geocoded day-by-day itineraries, forecasts weather with atmospheric radar, translates local menus via vision scanning, and computes live currency conversions in seconds.
How is TripMate AI different from ChatGPT for travel? ↓
Generic chatbots provide raw text ideas without real geographic verification. TripMate AI computes exact Haversine distances, runs TSP nearest-neighbor route optimization, checks live precipitation radar, renders interactive Leaflet map pins, and delivers real-time currency conversions.
Can I customize the itinerary after it is generated? ↓
Everything is customizable. You can add your own custom visiting spots, change trip durations, switch travel styles from Backpacker to Luxury, toggle dietary food filters, and mark places as visited on the interactive map.
Is there any upfront fee or booking pressure? ↓
There is zero upfront fee to plan. TripMate AI is completely free to customize and explore. You only pay for what you decide to book, with transparent pricing and no hidden markups.
Does the Audio Phrasebook support Indian languages? ↓
Yes. The Audio Phrasebook and Voice Translation Studio natively support Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Bengali, Marathi, and Urdu, alongside international languages like Japanese, French, and Spanish with phonetic guides and native browser audio synthesis.
